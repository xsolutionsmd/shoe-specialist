The shop

A neighborhood counter on Benfield Road

Shoe Specialist was established in 1978 and is owned by Coleman Eldridge. The shop sits in the Benfield Shopping Center and serves customers from across Anne Arundel County.

It is a practical, friendly repair counter where many customers keep coming back. Bring in the pair, the bag or the suitcase, and talk it through at the counter.
